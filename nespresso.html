Nespresso

A complete redesign of Nespresso’s websites and app worldwide, for consumers and business customers, built on a new design system.

The brief

Challenge

Redesign Nespresso’s global websites and app for both B2C and B2B customers.

Solution

A new platform that defines the new brand identity, backed by a complete design system. Our team of 4 UI designers and 2 UX designers produced more than 200 page designs.

The design gives Nespresso a fresh, bright and immersive look, and relies on AI-driven recommendations and personalisation based on each user’s preferences.

The project has not been publicly released.
